This Privacy Policy explains how CG QUIZZ MASTER 2.O ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App.

In brief: The App does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, home address, or similar personal identity details. Quiz content, bookmarks, scores, and study progress are stored locally on your device. The App displays advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), and Google may process device, network, and advertising information to provide and measure those ads.

1. Information You Provide

The App does not provide user registration, sign-in, or account creation. It does not ask you to submit your name, phone number, email address, home address, contacts, or payment information.

Quiz answers, bookmarks, study statistics, preferences, and other activity generated while using the App are stored locally on your device. The App's own code does not send this study information to a developer-operated server.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App uses local storage, including SQLite and app-private preferences. Depending on how you use the App, locally stored information may include:

  • Question bank content and practice-test information;
  • Quiz attempts, scores, correct-answer counts, and study statistics;
  • Bookmarks saved for revision;
  • App preferences, such as theme and exam profile; and
  • Locally maintained question-deletion and recovery metadata.

This information is stored in the App's private storage area. Based on the source-code audit, this locally stored study information is not transmitted by the App to us or to a third party. It may be lost if you clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove its local data.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. When ads are requested, displayed, or interacted with, Google may automatically collect or process information including, depending on your device, settings, region, and consent status:

  • IP address and information derived from it, such as approximate location;
  • Advertising ID or supported Android advertising identifiers, including Privacy Sandbox-related identifiers where available;
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating-system version, and network information;
  • Ad request, delivery, impression, click, and rewarded-ad interaction information; and
  • Ad performance, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals used by the advertising service.

This information may be processed for ad delivery, measurement, reporting, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's policies and applicable settings or consent choices. The App does not itself read or store the Advertising ID in its own application database.

We do not operate the advertising network and do not control Google's independent data practices or retention systems.

For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google advertising controls

Ad availability and whether an ad is personalized may depend on Google's systems, your device settings, consent choices, and laws applicable in your region. The App's use of AdMob does not mean every user will necessarily receive personalized advertising.

4. App Updates and Connectivity Checks

The App uses Google Play In-App Updates to check whether an update is available through Google Play. This process may involve the App's package name, version information, and device or Play Store compatibility information being processed by Google Play services.

The App also checks network reachability using a DNS lookup for google.com. This determines whether an internet connection is reachable; the App does not attach quiz answers, bookmarks, or study statistics to this check.

5. Information Not Collected Through Our Own App Code

Based on the source-code and Android configuration audit, the App does not include a developer-operated account system, custom backend, standalone analytics service, or dedicated crash-reporting service. The audit did not find App code requesting precise GPS location, contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, IMEI, or device serial number.

This statement concerns the App's own inspected code. It does not override or describe independent processing by Google Play, Android, AdMob, or other platform services. AdMob may process IP address, advertising identifiers, and advertising-related technical information as described above.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. The App does not send locally stored quiz scores, bookmarks, or study progress to a developer-operated server.

Information may be processed by Google through AdMob for advertising and by Google Play services for app-update functionality. The App's connectivity check also makes a DNS request for google.com through the device's configured DNS resolver.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Study information saved by the App remains in local App storage until you delete it, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or the device otherwise removes its local data. We do not maintain a developer-side copy of this local study information.

Information processed by Google AdMob or Google Play services is handled under Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. We do not control those systems; consult Google's privacy documentation and device/account controls for details.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is an educational quiz application and does not ask users to submit personal identity information. Advertising services may apply their own age-related, consent, and advertising rules.

Parents or guardians with questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's information practices may contact us using the details provided below.

9. Security

Study information is stored in the App's private local storage area. We take reasonable care in designing the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

10. Your Choices

  • You can remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
  • You can review or change advertising-related controls provided by Android and Google on your device, where available.
  • Where required, Google may present consent choices for advertising or related processing. Your choices and regional requirements may affect the ads and processing available to the App.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
